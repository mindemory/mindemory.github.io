MCN Alumni Network 2026

Methods in Computational Neuroscience (MCN) · MBL, Woods Hole, MA

An interactive connectivity graph of everyone who has passed through the MCN course at the Marine Biological Laboratory, students, faculty, directors, lecturers, and TAs. Nodes are people, edges are co-attendance (same cohort) and co-authorship (shared papers, via OpenAlex). Filter by cohort year, role, or research domain, switch between force, radial, and domain layouts, and click any person to see their trajectory.
