Tools for Labs

Thanos 2026

Early · In the browser, for any lab or group

A lab's memory. What a lab works out over the years, from the chart string and which grant pays for scans to who holds the lab card and where the code lives, kept in one place to search, and still there after the people who knew have moved on. It started for the Murray Lab at Dartmouth and the Clay Space Lab at NYU, and hasn't yet been tried by other labs.

Interactive Tools

MCN Alumni Network 2026

Methods in Computational Neuroscience (MCN) · MBL, Woods Hole, MA

An interactive connectivity graph of everyone who has passed through the MCN course at the Marine Biological Laboratory, students, faculty, directors, lecturers, and TAs. Nodes are people, edges are co-attendance (same cohort) and co-authorship (shared papers, via OpenAlex). Filter by cohort year, role, or research domain, switch between force, radial, and domain layouts in 2D or 3D, and click any person to see their trajectory.

Library Map 2026

My reading list · Rebuilt automatically from Paperpile

My Paperpile library, over a thousand papers, drawn as a map. On the topic map, each paper sits beside the papers most like it in title, keywords and abstract, and the clusters they form are named by their most telling words, from TMS and working memory capacity to traveling waves and pupil-linked arousal. A second map shows who writes with whom. Both work in 2D or 3D, and searching an author lights up all of their papers. The same code keeps my Notion reading list in step with Paperpile.
